# Visual study assets

`assets/bedtime-moon.png` was generated with the built-in image-generation tool
on 13 September 2026 and copied into this project. It is a concept asset, not
an approved final brand illustration. It is displayed decoratively in the demo.

## Complete generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: decorative header illustration for PeeLog,
a parent-facing nighttime logging app. Create one beautifully restrained bedtime
illustration: a small warm crescent moon resting on a softly folded blue pillow
with a loose slate-blue quilt beneath it, eyes closed as a tiny simple curved mark,
three very small muted stars nearby. Soft editorial gouache and cut-paper shapes,
gentle rounded contours, subtle dry-brush grain, sophisticated and comforting, not
cartoonish or babyish. Landscape composition, single small vignette centered with
ample empty space around it, no full scene. Dark solid midnight background #10151e
all the way to every edge, so it blends into an app interface. Palette: muted slate
blue, grey lavender, warm desaturated oatmeal crescent, no bright whites or glow.
Low luminance designed for a dark bedroom. No text, letters, logos, UI, border,
gradients, or watermark. The illustration should be visually quiet and remain
understandable displayed at 180 pixels wide.

## Interface icons

The small interface icons are local, authored SVG symbols in `index.html`. They
are exploratory functional marks; all action icons have accompanying text labels.
No external fonts, icon packages, analytics, or services load in this demo.
